☣ MAN'S LAST STAND

The last laboratory

The sirens stopped on the ninth day. The radio stopped on the twelfth. By the third week even the screaming had run out, and the only sounds left in the city were the ones the dead made. As far as you know, you are the last living person in the world.

You were never a hero. You were a researcher, and the lab beneath the old hospital was the one place nobody thought to loot. The doors are sealed, the generators still hum, and the cold room is full of what the staff left behind: a collection of cadavers, surgeons and soldiers, athletes and thieves, the gentle and the monstrous, each one waiting under a numbered tag.

Buried in the notes is a set of instructions for making control fluid, a serum that tells the dead what to do. The first page is stained with something dark. The last page is torn out. Nobody who wrote it is around to explain what the missing page said.

The rest is simple and terrible. Choose a cadaver. Choose the life it led before the end. Pour the fluid, and it stands up and obeys. Send it into the ruins of the Wasteland, the Mall, the Shelter, the Ghetto, the Military Base, the Museum and the Lab to bring back the weapons, armor, specimens and scrap you need to raise the next one.

The dead are everywhere, and they are not waiting. Neither are you. You can build an army of them and take the world back, or one day you may find yourself becoming just another body in the collection.

Will you survive the apocalypse, or join it?

How it works
  • Forge: choose a brain and a body type, and raise an undead.
  • Undead: check their stats, body, equipment and backstory.
  • Expeditions: send a party of up to 3 into the ruins for loot and specimens.
  • Storage: equip, upgrade, scrap or gamble what you find.
  • Morgue: your cadaver and brain stock.
